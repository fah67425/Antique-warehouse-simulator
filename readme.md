# The Antique Corner

The Antique Corner is a browser-based antique shop simulator built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Buy antiques, manage your collection, serve customers, complete challenges, earn coins, upgrade your shop, and build a better reputation.

## Features

- Antique shop simulator gameplay
- 8 collectible antique items
- Buy and sell system
- Fixed selling prices
- Collection and warehouse system
- Random daily challenges
- Challenge rewards
- Random customer names
- Customer ordering system
- Customers only request unlocked items
- Customer reactions
- Customer deadlines
- Random 15 to 30 second customer timer
- Customer reviews
- 1 to 5 star review ratings
- Reviews based on service speed
- Shop progression system

## Shop Levels

### Level 1
- 4 available antique items
- Normal customer rewards

### Level 2
- 6 available antique items
- Unlocks 2 additional items
- Customer rewards increase by 20%

### Level 3
- All 8 antique items available
- Unlocks the final 2 items
- Customer rewards increase by 40% total

## Shop Upgrades

Each upgrade normally costs 150 coins.

Upgrading the shop:
- Unlocks additional antiques
- Increases customer rewards
- Progresses the shop toward Level 3

## Customer System

Customers randomly request antiques that are currently available at the player's shop level.

Customers also have a limited amount of time to receive their requested item.

The timer randomly starts between 15 and 30 seconds.

If the order is completed quickly, the customer gives a better review.

If the timer expires, the customer leaves and gives a bad review.

## Reviews

Customers can leave reviews from 1 to 5 stars.

Reviews depend on how quickly the player completes an order.

Fast service gives better reviews.

Slow service gives lower reviews.

Reviews appear in the Customer Reviews section, with the newest review appearing first.

## Challenges

The game generates random daily challenges.

Examples include:
- Buying multiple antiques
- Collecting different antiques
- Spending coins
- Buying specific antiques

Completing challenges gives additional coins.

## Audio

The game uses the Web Audio API for audio.

Included sounds:
- Background music
- Coin sound
- Cash/order sound
- Upgrade sounds
- Other gameplay sound effects

The game does not require external audio files for these sounds.
